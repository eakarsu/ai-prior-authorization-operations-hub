# AI Prior Authorization Operations Hub Feature Registry

- `auth-intake` - Authorization Intake (Intake): Patient, provider, payer, service request, diagnosis, and submission status.
- `payer-rule-matching` - Payer Rule Matching (Rules): Medical policy criteria, covered indications, documentation requirements, and rule fit.
- `evidence-checklist` - Evidence Checklist (Evidence): Clinical notes, imaging, labs, prior therapy, contraindications, and missing proof.
- `packet-generation` - Packet Generation (Submission): Cover letters, forms, evidence bundle, coding details, and submission readiness.
- `denial-prevention` - Denial Prevention (Risk): Likely denial reasons, weak evidence, payer history, and prevention actions.
- `appeal-routing` - Appeal Routing (Appeals): Denied requests, appeal deadlines, reviewer owner, letter draft, and escalation status.
- `peer-review-prep` - Peer Review Prep (Clinical Review): Peer-to-peer talking points, clinical rationale, policy gaps, and supporting facts.
- `sla-tracking` - SLA Tracking (Operations): Payer response timers, urgent flags, backlog, overdue work, and team capacity.
- `authorization-analytics` - Authorization Analytics (Reporting): Approval rates, denial trends, payer performance, turnaround, and leakage.
- `patient-updates` - Patient Updates (Communications): Patient-facing status, next steps, delay reasons, and communication log.
- `documents` - Documents
- `notifications` - Notifications
- `integrations` - Integrations
- `profiles` - Profiles
- `ai-assistant` - AI Assistant
- `ai-tools` - AI Tools
